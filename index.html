Calculation formulas and research assumptions
θ = atan2(√(Ax² + Ay²), |Az|) × 180/π
Δθ = |θ(current) − θ(baseline)|
Rθ = |θ(current) − θ(previous)| / Δt (hours)
Aθ = (signed rate(current) − signed rate(previous)) / Δt (hours)
T = min(1, Δθ / 3°)
V = min(1, Rθ / 0.1°/h)
A = min(1, max(0, Aθ) / 0.05°/h²)
LGMI = 100 × (0.3T + 0.5V + 0.2A)

Weights and 3° / 0.05°/h² reference values are illustrative and unvalidated. The 0.1°/h rate is a research reference, not a universal alarm threshold. Baseline is the earliest available real reading in loaded Firebase history; recalibrate for deployment. Rates are not computed from copied, estimated or simulated records. Only quasi-static, correctly mounted accelerometers can estimate inclination; single-axis inclination magnitude cannot detect every type of slope movement.

LGMI is not a landslide probability or a validated safety classification. It does not generate emergency alerts.

LRI Methodology & Validation Reference
The weighted-index formula combines load departure (40%), acceleration departure (30%), rainfall (20%), and soil moisture (10%) scaled against empirical 95th percentiles from reference baselines. The resulting score is an authoritative physical sensor index adapted from Das et al.
